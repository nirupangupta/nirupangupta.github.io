Customer problem

Businesses lose opportunities when calls go unanswered, repetitive questions consume staff time, or service requests are not routed to the right team. Ralvie.AI addresses these workflows with AI voice and receptionist capabilities.

My role

SaaS Sales / AI Solutions. My work spans customer discovery, requirements, solution design, demonstrations, proposals, product configuration, implementation, onboarding and post-sale engagement.

What I do

Translate customer problems into AI/SaaS workflows; configure AI voice-agent accounts and receptionist call flows; define routing and deployment requirements; discuss APIs/integrations; work with developers and UI/UX teams on product feedback and feature requirements; and contribute to pricing, competitive analysis and GTM strategy.

The product is used for international SaaS sales across the US and Canada, with use cases spanning hospitality, automotive, restaurants and storage.
