Problem

Lenovo needed stronger frontline sales engagement and product adoption for MBS products. The solution needed a clear incentive workflow while protecting the program from duplicate submissions and incentive leakage.

My role

Key Account Manager · Project owner / solution lead. I identified the business need, gathered requirements from Lenovo stakeholders and shaped the incentive workflow with the delivery teams.

What I did

Designed the incentive workflow; coordinated data/API requirements; supported partner onboarding, sales verification, creatives and payment implementation; and designed validation around unique order numbers to prevent duplicate submissions and incentive leakage.

Outcome

The program reached 500+ partners, generated 450+ registrations and 250+ first-month conversions across 4,000+ stores/users. The project description reports approximately ₹20 Cr in quarterly Lenovo revenue contribution and 113% sales uplift against a 50% target.
