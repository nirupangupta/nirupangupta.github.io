Problem

Western Digital MEA retailers had a product knowledge and engagement gap. The existing TechTalks program in India had already demonstrated the value of structured product education, creating an opportunity to adapt the model for MEA.

My role

Key Account Manager · Project Owner. I identified the regional need during a weekly discussion with the WD India Sales Head, gathered requirements, understood regional product and training needs, pitched the MEA expansion to WD leadership and drove the project through launch.

What I did

I coordinated with WD product, development and UI/UX teams to configure the Bsharp SaaS/LMS platform; structured product-training modules; managed content requirements; coordinated client meetings; onboarded users; tracked reporting and analytics; and led the launch.

We launched first in UAE in October 2022, followed by Africa. The platform was delivered across Android and iOS.

Outcome

The MEA rollout supported 130+ retailers and extended the TechTalks model into a new regional market.

The strongest part of the project was connecting a customer-observed problem to a concrete product expansion and taking it from requirements to launch.
